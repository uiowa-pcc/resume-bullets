Build a Strong Resume Bullet

Start with one thing you actually did. Add the details that show how, why, for whom, or at what scale—and turn it into a concise resume bullet.

You do not need a dramatic accomplishment or a perfect number. Specific, accurate details are more useful than inflated language.

Use the formula as a guide, not a requirement. A strong starting point is: action verb + what you did + useful detail about how, why, scope, or result.
